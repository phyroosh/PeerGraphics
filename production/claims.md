# Claim Ledger — aMCC and Willpower

| ID | Claim used in narration | Type / strength | Support | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C01 | The aMCC is involved in evaluating whether an effortful goal is worth pursuing. | Interpretation supported by converging evidence | Touroutoglou et al., *The Tenacious Brain* (2020), [PMC7381101](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7381101/) | The region is part of a distributed system; it is not a solitary “willpower center.” |
| C02 | aMCC integrates signals relevant to motivation, cognitive control, and bodily resource regulation. | Review synthesis | Kleckner et al., *Motivation in the Service of Allostasis* (2019), [PMC6884085](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6884085/) | “Integrates” describes a model built from multiple lines of evidence, not a direct readout of a single mental state. |
| C03 | Effort allocation can be framed as balancing expected reward with control costs. | Computational account | Shenhav et al., *Toward a Rational and Mechanistic Account of Mental Effort* (2017), [Annual Reviews](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-neuro-072116-031526) | This is a useful framework, not proof that every decision is literally a conscious calculation. |
| C04 | Reducing friction, making progress visible, and providing meaningful rewards are practical behavior-design strategies. | Practical recommendation | Framed as environmental support for action; no claim that these techniques enlarge or “train” aMCC. | Effects vary by person and context; this is not medical advice. |

Deliberately excluded claims: that willpower is stored like a battery, that aMCC is a single switch, or that deliberate discomfort makes aMCC physically grow/shrink in a daily cycle. Those stronger statements are not supported by the above evidence at the certainty required here.
