# Research & Claims Ledger: Identifying Healthy vs Unhealthy Relationships

| Claim ID | Claim Statement | Psychological & Clinical Support | Limits & Nuances | Script Placement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **C01** | Intense emotional chemistry and volatility are frequently mistaken for deep compatibility. | Attachment Theory (Hazan & Shaver, 1987); intermittent reinforcement creates dopamine spikes resembling obsession rather than genuine relational security. | High passion can co-exist with health, but without safety it devolves into trauma-bonding. | Scene 1 (0–377f) |
| **C02** | The true determinant of relational health is emotional safety during conflict. | Dr. John Gottman (*The Seven Principles for Making Marriage Work*); psychological safety (Edmondson, 1999). | Disagreements are normal; the differentiating variable is regulation vs. attack. | Scene 1, Scene 4 (284f, 898f) |
| **C03** | In unhealthy dynamics, conflict is a struggle for dominance/power where boundaries are perceived as personal rejection. | Structural Family Therapy (Minuchin); Coercive Control Dynamics (Stark, 2007). | Early boundary-crossing can be subtle (e.g., guilt trips rather than overt physical aggression). | Scene 2 (377–668f) |
| **C04** | Disagreements penalized with guilt, the silent treatment (stonewalling), or explosive defensiveness lead to hypervigilance. | Gottman's "Four Horsemen" (Criticism, Contempt, Defensiveness, Stonewalling); autonomic nervous system hypervigilance. | Occasional defensive reactions happen; persistent patterns define toxicity. | Scene 2, Scene 3 (552f, 668f) |
| **C05** | Healthy conflict replaces contempt with curiosity and aligns partners against the shared obstacle. | Gottman Institute research on Attunement; Collaborative Problem Solving. | Requires mutual willingness; one partner cannot unilaterally create a safe dynamic. | Scene 4, Scene 5 (977f, 1078f) |
| **C06** | Setting a boundary is an immediate diagnostic test of relational safety. | Boundary Psychology (Cloud & Townsend; Nedra Glover Tawwab). | A healthy partner may initially feel disappointed, but respects the limit and does not retaliate. | Scene 6, Scene 7 (1238–1824f) |
