# Production Brief: Willpower & aMCC Daily Renewal

## Topic & Core Premise
Consistency and Renewal: The process of building willpower requires daily effort. Just like overcoming addiction, it must be renewed every single day; otherwise, the anterior midcingulate cortex (aMCC) can shrink.

## Core Audience & Tone
- Audience: Intellectually curious viewers interested in psychology, self-improvement, neuroscience, and mental resilience.
- Tone: Punchy, authoritative, grounded in neuroscience, conversational, non-dogmatic.

## Target Format
- 9:16 Vertical Video (1080 × 1920 pixels)
- 30 fps, 1710 frames (57.0 seconds total, matching the 56.76s ElevenLabs audio)
- Styling: Theme 09 (Punk Editorial & Risograph Collage) with open vector canvas, living background, and physical vector mechanisms.
