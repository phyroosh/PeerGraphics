# Moodboard & Atmospheric Art Direction: Whispering Woods

## 1. Setting & Mood
A mystical, ancient coniferous forest at deep dusk. The atmosphere shifts from cold, silent isolation to warm magical awakening when the lantern ignites.

## 2. Color Script
- **Shot 1 (The Cold Gathering Dusk)**:
  - Deep Twilight Sky: `#0B132B` to `#1C2541` gradient.
  - Distant Mist / Fog: `#3A506B` with 25% opacity.
  - Silhouetted Pines: `#0D1B2A` and `#1B263B`.
  - Mossy Forest Bed: `#1E3F20` to `#122613`.
  - Ambient Color Grade: Cool blue/indigo vignette.
- **Shot 2 (The Golden Flare & Awakening)**:
  - Ember Core: `#FFFFFF` (radiant center) to `#FFE066` to `#FF9F1C`.
  - Volumetric Lantern Halo: Radial gradient `#FFD166` (opacity $0.45 \to 0.00$, radius $600\text{px}$).
  - Character Rim Light: Soft golden highlights on coat edges and hair strands.

## 3. Multi-Plane Layer Breakdown
1. **Plane 0: Far Horizon ($Z=3.0$)**: Night sky, crescent moon, stars, low blue fog. Parallax factor: $0.12$.
2. **Plane 1: Midground Forest ($Z=1.8$)**: Layered pine tree silhouettes, drifting mist layers, ambient firefly particles. Parallax factor: $0.45$.
3. **Plane 2: Action Ground ($Z=1.0$)**: Mossy clearing, ancient boulder, path. Parallax factor: $1.00$.
4. **Plane 3: Foreground Framing ($Z=0.5$)**: Overhanging silhouetted pine needles and ivy vines. Parallax factor: $1.40$.
