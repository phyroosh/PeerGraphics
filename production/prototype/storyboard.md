# Storyboard: The Lantern Keeper (Prototype MMP)

## Story Synopsis
In the heart of the whispering woods at dusk, Leo searches for the legendary lost ember. Holding an unlit brass lantern, he pauses, sensing something in the shadows. When the lantern suddenly blazes to life, casting golden radiance through the trees, he discovers he is not alone.

## Scene 1: The Whispering Woods

### Shot 1: The Gathering Dusk (Establishing Mood & Character State)
- **Shot ID**: `S01_SH01`
- **Frames**: 0 to 180 (6.00s @ 30 fps)
- **Narration Line**: *"In the heart of the whispering woods, Leo searched for the lost ember."*
- **Framing**: Wide Cinematic (Full Body Character in Environment).
- **Camera Movement**: Slow, continuous push-in zoom ($1.00 \to 1.08$) with subtle horizontal parallax tracking ($x: 0 \to -40\text{px}$).
- **Environment**:
  - *Far Background ($Z=3.0$)*: Indigo twilight sky, sliver of silver moon, twinkling stars, soft blue fog bank.
  - *Midground ($Z=1.8$)*: Dense pine tree silhouettes, drifting mist, glowing teal fireflies.
  - *Ground Plane ($Z=1.0$)*: Ancient mossy knoll, cobblestone path, cold stone slab.
  - *Foreground ($Z=0.5$)*: Dark overhanging pine branches, creeping ivy with depth blur.
- **Character Action**:
  - Leo stands in 3/4 profile holding an unlit antique lantern.
  - Idle breathing cycle (chest elevation + shoulder sway).
  - Eyes scan the horizon (gaze tracks from right to center), followed by natural eye blinks.
  - Scarf and coat tails flap gently in the evening wind.
- **Emotional Beat**: Mystery, quiet solitude, cautious anticipation.
- **Transition**: Direct cut on action to Shot 2 at Frame 180.

---

### Shot 2: The Spark of Wonder (Emotional Reaction & Discovery)
- **Shot ID**: `S01_SH02`
- **Frames**: 180 to 360 (6.00s @ 30 fps)
- **Narration Line**: *"When the lantern flared, he realized he wasn't alone."*
- **Framing**: Medium Close-Up (Waist-Up Character Focus).
- **Camera Movement**: Sudden micro-shake at Frame 230 on flare impact ($3\text{px}$ spring damping), then slow settling hold.
- **Environment**:
  - Warm lighting bloom transforming the dark woods into amber-gilded foliage.
  - Floating luminous embers drifting upwards.
- **Character Action**:
  - Frames 180–225: Leo lifts the lantern toward chest height, peering curiously into the glass.
  - Frame 230: The ember bursts to life inside the lantern!
  - Frames 230–280: Volumetric golden illumination floods across Leo's face. His head recoils slightly with spring anticipation, eyes widen with awe, eyebrows rise, mouth parts in a gasp of wonder.
  - Frames 280–360: Leo settles into an expression of radiant fascination, breathing softly as golden light glints in his eyes.
- **Emotional Beat**: Sudden wonder, revelation, magical awakening.
- **Transition**: Concluding fade to warm dusk.
