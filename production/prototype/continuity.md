# Continuity Ledger: The Lantern Keeper

## 1. Spatial Geography
- **Screen Direction**: Leo is facing towards screen Right throughout both Shot 1 and Shot 2.
- **Lantern Placement**: Leo holds the brass lantern in his right hand (downwards at side in Shot 1, raised to mid-chest height in Shot 2).
- **Light Source**:
  - Shot 1: Dim cool ambient moonlight from top-left (45° angle).
  - Shot 2: Dim cool ambient moonlight until Frame 230, when the lantern becomes the dominant, intense warm point light source originating from the right side of the frame at waist/chest height.

## 2. Wardrobe & Prop Integrity
- **Scarf**: Red wool scarf wrapped once around neck with trailing end over right shoulder. Must be identical in color (`#C0392B`), width, and texture across cuts.
- **Spectacles**: Round brass spectacles with double circular rims and bridge.
- **Lantern**: Hexagonal antique brass cage with pyramidal roof, loop handle, and transparent glass panels. Unlit in Shot 1, ignited in Shot 2.
