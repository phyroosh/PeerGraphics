# Character bible — Mira

Mira is a 1:4.5 storybook runner and field researcher: round rust-red hood, indigo vest, oat shirt, moss trousers, amber compass. Her silhouette is the oversized hood and small glowing chest-strap. Palette: skin `#B96F52`, hair `#38251F`, hood `#B8543D`, vest `#263C59`, scarf `#E7B65D`, ground light `#F6D78B`.

Rig: root → hips → torso; torso → neck → head → eyes/pupils/lids/mouth; torso → left/right shoulder → elbow → hand; hips → thighs → calves → boots. Pivots are explicit at shoulder, elbow, hip, knee, and neck. Idle acting uses chest breathing, eye saccades, blinks, and a delayed scarf sway. States: wary, braced, reflective, determined, relieved.
