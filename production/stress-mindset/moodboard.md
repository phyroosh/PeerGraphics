# Moodboard — The Choice Garden

The world begins in rain-blue pre-dawn (`#17324A`) with a violet storm; forced pressure is cool, narrow, and mechanical. Choice is not a magical escape: it is a warm gold path revealed within the same landscape. The final palette is apricot dawn (`#F6C878`) against deep pine.

Four planes: far sky and weather; mid layered pines; ground trail/conveyor; soft, blurred foreground reeds. Parallax runs at 0.12, 0.45, 1.0, and 1.4 times the motivated camera pan. Dust/rain and light motes maintain atmosphere.
