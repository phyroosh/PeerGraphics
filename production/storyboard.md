- **Audio**: "When practicing a skill, doing the exact same drill a hundred times feels productive. But your brain actually learns best when you never repeat the exact same movement."

  - At f155 ("never repeat"), a small obstacle / perturbation shifts into the rail. The rigid rail jams with violent stress fractures (`#FF0055`), and a jagged "BRITTLE" alert stamps down.
  - Passes Mute Test: Viewer sees rigid rote tracks breaking instantly when conditions change.

## Scene 02: Bernstein & The Blacksmith's Discovery (Frames 311 – 814 | 10.37s – 27.14s)
- **Audio**: "In the nineteen thirties, neurophysiologist Nikolai Bernstein studied master blacksmiths. The hammer hit the exact same millimeter every time, yet the path of their arms and joints was different on every single strike. He called this repetition without repetition."
- **Visual Action**:

  - Three consecutive strikes crash down: each strike creates seismic shockwaves and sparks (`#FFE600`, `#FF0055`).
  - High-speed cyclography lines illuminate in 3 distinct colors (Neon Yellow, Electric Cyan, Hot Pink), tracing 3 completely different spatial paths through the air.
  - Close-up zooms into the anvil: all 3 tracer lines terminate on the exact same glowing focal target millimeter crosshair (`TARGET: 0.00 mm ERROR`).
  - Tactile Risograph stamp slams down: "REPETITION WITHOUT REPETITION" with offset drop shadow.

## Scene 03: Neural Plasticity vs Autopilot (Frames 814 – 1189 | 27.14s – 39.63s)
- **Audio**: "Your brain does not wire rigid, robotic tracks. Neuroplasticity works by solving problems. When you mindlessly repeat an identical motion, your nervous system goes on autopilot and stops adapting."
- **Visual Action**:
  - Anatomical vector brain cortex profile.
  - Side A: Monotonous identical rep loops in a faded gray loop with flatline ECG waves. A stark warning sticker stamps down: "AUTOPILOT: 0% ADAPTATION".
  - Side B: Adaptive problem-solving triggers vibrant electric cyan and neon yellow synaptic bursts, dendrites branching out and seeking solutions.
  - Contrast proves that the brain changes through active challenge, not rote routine.

## Scene 04: Dynamic Problem Solving (Frames 1189 – 1332 | 39.63s – 44.42s)
- **Audio**: "True mastery is training your brain to find the solution through changing conditions."
- **Visual Action**:
